    Proof, not claims

    Not what it says.
    What it did.

    A spawn does not stop at saying it is done. It hands back proof you can check, and you answer on the proof itself. Your notes go back to that spawn, or start a new one, and the next round begins from them.

    1. 1It does the work
    2. 2It hands back proof
    3. 3You mark it up
    4. 4Your notes go back

    • FilmsThe workflows it changed, filmed start to end.

    • Manual testIt clicks through like a person and boxes each fault.
    • Postman runEvery request and check, passed or failed.
    • Rules reviewIts changes checked against your own rules.
    • Change storyThe change walked through, scene by scene.
